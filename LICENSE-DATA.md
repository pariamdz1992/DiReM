## Third-party sources

- **Transmitter records:** derived from ISED's *Terrestrial Spectrum Licence Site Data Extract*
  (Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada).
- **Basemap:** the basemap in the exported images (and in input channel 0) is rendered from
  OpenStreetMap data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License
  (ODbL), <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.
- **Propagation and map data:** coverage was computed with ATDI HTZ Communications, using terrain,
  clutter and building data from ATDI's map database.
